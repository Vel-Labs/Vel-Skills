# Evidence-backed resume tailoring

## Purpose

Tailor an evidence-backed resume to a target role.

## Inputs

- Candidate profile, approved resume and any benchmark, target role, and applicable disclosure limits.

## Outputs

- A versioned resume and inspected export with evidence and unresolved questions recorded.

## Non-goals

- Submitting applications without authorization or inventing claims.

Use for a targeted resume, benchmark comparison, or resume-process improvement. Follow the client's current workspace rules, approved baseline, source profile and application boundaries. Do not infer submission authority from a writing request.

## Start from evidence

Read the compact candidate profile and current approved baseline first, then the job description and relevant source material. If a client has a benchmark resume, use it as a quality reference; later candidate corrections still govern the facts. Read the applicable client guidance and the profile owner’s question and approval records.

Build a compact responsibility-to-evidence map. For each major requirement, identify the candidate's action, users, implementation or decision, result, source and remaining limits. Distinguish direct candidate testimony, resume-reported detail, repository implementation, observed behavior and adoption. A codebase or test pass alone does not establish personal authorship, real use, business value or deployment.

Recover earlier answers and relevant operational records before asking again. For project use, inspect actual execution/results when available; skill inventories, search hits and self-reported receipt IDs alone do not prove execution. Choose projects for ownership, real use and a concrete outcome before keyword similarity.

## Interview only the missing part

Reuse existing question IDs and lifecycle rules. Ask one concrete question at a time when the candidate prefers that cadence: a real request, personal decision, failed case, correction or outcome. Acknowledge “not measured” as a valid limit. Do not keep seeking a number after the bounded story is adequately supported.

Record a new answer in the existing source/profile/reusable-answer owners before closing its question. Keep exact employer wording and candidate-selected options attached to that application. Choosing the closest multiple-choice method does not establish every implied historical measurement or authorize new resume metrics. Reopen only the affected scope for a materially new requirement or correction.

## Tailor without losing substance

Preserve the approved baseline's factual and structural constraints while adapting emphasis, ordering and accurate terminology to the job. Prefer details that demonstrate how the work operated: a migration, source structure, integration boundary, maintenance cadence, handoff, test, review gate or before/after result.

Before finalizing, compare the derivative against both the job and the benchmark. Look specifically for useful details lost during compression. Restore a supported detail when it directly demonstrates a major requirement, preferably within an existing bullet. Do not replace implementation evidence with generic skills language or add a keyword that overstates the underlying work.

Separate reach from repeat use; a cohort from enterprise deployment; fewer revisions from measured hours or ROI; personal tooling use from customer adoption; and future integration from delivered capability. Preserve names/titles/dates and disclosure limits. Keep prototypes and deployed work distinct.

## Review and export

Use the current document/PDF skills and the client's existing exporter/template. Save a new version instead of overwriting a final artifact. Review the changed claims, render and inspect every page, compare exported body text with the approved source, and check links. Keep review receipts proportional: source and export hashes, meaningful limits and the selected version are enough for an ordinary derivative.

Update the profile hub and variant registry so humans and agents reach the same current files. Preserve previous approvals as history and exact submitted artifacts. Do not upgrade optional evidence gaps into automatic blockers or claim an ATS parse from a PDF text extraction.

## Application handoff

Pass the exact reviewed PDF and source revision into the existing application workflow if one is supplied. Revalidate the live role and required question options; retain discovery, quality approval, preparation, submission and confirmation as separate states. Use current official sources where possible and label employer-posted secondary evidence. An application form may pose a materially narrower claim than a resume bullet; resolve that field rather than changing the candidate's history to fit the preferred answer.

Retain newly learned reusable process decisions in this skill and client-specific facts in the client workspace. Do not create a new framework, script, metric or question bank when an existing one already owns the job.

## Workflow

Work through the evidence, interview, tailoring, and export stages below.

## Checks

- Compare against the role and approved baseline. Render each page and verify text and links.

## Failure modes & recovery

If a claim cannot be supported, keep its limitation explicit. If the pipeline is unavailable, hand off the exact reviewed artifact and source revision.
