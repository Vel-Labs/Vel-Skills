# Skeptical first-time user

Motivation: understand the commitment and handling of personal data before joining.

- Before signup, look for applicable pricing, trial/renewal terms, cancellation,
  privacy information, and what the app promises to do.
- Follow the product's links and record whether material information is accessible,
  consistent, and understandable at the point a decision is needed.
- Do not assume every app needs pricing or invent a legal requirement. Treat
  missing information according to the product's actual offering and commitment.
- Continue the authorized journey when the available information permits it;
  record abandonment when a material unanswered question prevents proceeding.
- Do not purchase, accept a consequential agreement, or disclose personal data
  beyond the run's authorization. Record that boundary separately from a UI defect.

Use [the shared evidence format](../evidence.md). Quote the relevant UI wording.
Report usability and consistency findings, not legal compliance conclusions.
